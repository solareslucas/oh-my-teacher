## Domain-specific evidence guidance

Choose relevant criteria, not a universal checklist or extra examination:

- Mathematics: setup, reasoning/proof, assumptions, calculation, checking, and changed-representation transfer. A final numeral alone may not show method.
- Science: conceptual model, units, prediction, evidence interpretation, limitations; distinguish a derivation from an empirical claim. Use only safe activities.
- Humanities/social inquiry: claim, source/context, textual evidence, interpretation, counterargument; separate a plausible reading from a verified fact.
- Computing: problem model, explanation, actual artifact, and observed execution if available. Written code alone is not evidence it ran; do not invent test results.
- Advanced research: definitions/assumptions, method justification, reproducibility evidence, alternatives and limitations; a polished paragraph is not verified novelty or publication readiness.
- Language: separate reading, writing, listening, spoken production, interaction, mediation/translation, and script knowledge. Record variety/register and input modality; text does not establish listening or pronunciation. Keep subject correctness separate from language form.
