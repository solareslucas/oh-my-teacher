# Research record

Use only when the learner explicitly requests an artifact or save. This is a blank record structure, not evidence that research occurred. Keep it in chat unless durable persistence is explicitly consented and a learner is selected through `/teach-profile`; save only inside that learner's `.teacher/learners/<alias>/` directory, normally `artifacts/`. Omit irrelevant sections rather than fabricating entries. Mark unknown, unavailable, and planned work explicitly. Do not include participant identifiers or unnecessary sensitive data.

- Working title, record date/version, explanation language:
- Question, audience/use, learner goal:
- Research type and why it fits:
- Status: exploration / bounded narrative review / protocol or proposal / actual study report / conceptual argument / other accurately named stage:
- Actual work completed versus planned work:
- Available tools/materials and offline/access limits:
- Actual observations/data versus explicitly illustrative/simulated material:
- Candidate contribution; novelty unestablished unless adequately checked:

## Scope and plan

- Key concepts/definitions, population or context, outcomes/time, assumptions:
- Inclusion/exclusion criteria and reasons; dates, languages, material types:
- Evidence needed and why synthesis, primary evidence, or both fit:
- Search platforms and planned stopping/scope limits:
- Deviations from plan and reasons, with dates:
- Ethics/review requirements, consent/assent/permissions and current status before collection:
- Privacy, storage, access, sharing rights, and data minimization:

## Search log

## Source register

Access status: full text opened / abstract only / supplied excerpt only / metadata only / unavailable. Specify sections actually inspected; do not equate “full text opened” with “all pages read.” Snippets are discovery only. Record unknown metadata instead of guessing it. List status checks not performed as unresolved.

| Source ID | Verified citation, stable URL/identifier and version | Access date/status; sections read | Design/material, population/context, measures | Methods, bias/funding and applicability limits | Independent corroboration, shared data/reports, counterevidence | Dated version/correction/retraction checks actually made; unresolved checks |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Claim provenance ledger

Use stable IDs such as C1 and S1. Evidence kind: sourced claim / derivation / inference / unknown / explicitly illustrative. Record supporting and opposing sources. A locator is an observed page, section, table, or paragraph anchor, not a guessed page number. Label paraphrases and translations; do not fabricate quotes. Confidence is a scoped explanation, not a numerical trust score or absolute verdict.

| Claim ID | Exact bounded claim and evidence kind | Source ID(s) | Opened passage/brief faithful paraphrase and locator/link | Access status | Contradictions, limitations, missing checks | Confidence and reasons; what could change it |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Synthesis or actual findings

- Supported answer, linked to claim IDs and source passages:
- Agreements and genuinely independent support:
- Contradictions and possible reasons, without suppressing inconvenient findings:
- Applicability, precision, bias, access and review-process limits:
- What remains unknown; learner's next question/activity:

Only report findings actually obtained. For a protocol/proposal, replace this section with **Planned analysis and decision criteria** in future tense; hypotheses or expected outcomes are not results. Do not infer actual findings from illustrative data.

## Methods and reproducibility

- Actual methods, provenance/data versions, sampling and measurements, units/data dictionary:
- Transformations, exclusions, missingness, analyses actually executed:
- Planned methods/analysis separately, with rationale and feasibility:
- Relevant known tool/code versions, seeds, assumptions, and robustness checks:
- Real availability locations and restrictions for data, code, extraction forms, and materials:
- Actual registration/protocol status, amendments, and dates; do not invent registrations or links:

## Writing and human review

- Intended genre, audience, assignment/publication constraints:
- Supported draft sections and claim-ID links; unsupported claims removed or marked as questions/inferences:

- Funding/conflicts, contributors, AI assistance disclosure and rights as applicable:
- Human content/methods/ethics review needed before publication:
- No external submission, contact, registration, or publication without separate explicit approval for that action/destination:
