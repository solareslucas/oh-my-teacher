# Changelog

## 2026-10-24

- Added an OMP-native adaptive teacher/research-mentor role, sticky safeguards, and project-local privacy defaults without SDK usage, hooks, runtime dependencies, or a pinned model.
- Added `adaptive-tutor`, `language-coach`, and `research-methods` skills, with eleven `/teach-*` command entry points.
- Added manageable lessons, homework rubrics, evidence-aware feedback/reports, manual retrieval review, and separate subject/language/modality evidence.
- Added opt-in pseudonymous learner profiles, scoped consent, corrections, local forgetting, selected-record continuity, and Git-ignored private storage.
- Added source-access distinctions, reproducible search records, claim-level provenance, bias/applicability/version checks, and honest paper/protocol workflows with human review.
- Added blank record templates and setup, teaching, research, architecture, privacy, and observed-verification guides.
- Corrected profile operations to stop after profile management instead of silently beginning a lesson; verified the corrected behavior through OMP.
- Added `/teach-typst`, which compiles (or drafts and compiles) a Typst document from the selected, consenting learner's `artifacts/typst/` into `artifacts/pdf/` with `--root` confined to that learner directory; verified compile, path-traversal rejection, and no-alias behavior through OMP.
- Rebuilt homework around a bounded evidence scan (`docs/homework-evidence.md`): sections A–F chosen from the learner's evidence (retrieval, faded worked examples, interleaving, explanation, guided investigation, self-assessment), an explicit delivery format (first attempt without notes or AI, reasoning submitted, format matching the target assessment), assignment IDs, and no answers in assignments.
- Added subject profiles (`.omp/skills/adaptive-tutor/references/subjects.md`) defining what to submit, how it is checked, needed modality, and what the tutor cannot observe, for mathematics, proofs, programming, languages, labs, writing, history, art/design, music, clinical, and motor skills.
- Added a learner-set study plan to the profile template; a recorded homework expectation is followed without re-asking until changed.
- Added `templates/assignments.md`, `templates/typst/homework.typ`, the `/teach-resume` command and **Resume** workflow, and homework drafting in `/teach-typst`.

## 2026-10-25

- `adaptive-tutor` now calibrates support per component rather than per person: Intake notes which prior knowledge from an adjacent area transfers and where rules differ; Lesson gives worked examples for new tools/notation in a different context while leaving already demonstrated parts to the learner, treats naming a tool as a pointer rather than an example, and checks carried-over habits with predict-then-explain items; homework section B applies fading per component. Documented in `docs/teaching.md`; evidence and its limits (an extension of the expertise-reversal source, not a direct finding) in `docs/homework-evidence.md`; verified through OMP with fictional scenarios.

## 2026-10-26

- `adaptive-tutor` now requires examples, hints, and starter files to differ from the task in solution structure, not just in names: if renaming an example's identifiers, values, or nouns produces the solution, or it follows the task's step sequence, the tutor cuts it down to the isolated new component or changes the task. Success on a structurally identical parallel problem (section B) is recorded as supported, and project deliverables never get a structural twin. Documented in `docs/teaching.md`; the rule is labeled a design inference in `docs/homework-evidence.md`; checked through OMP with two fictional scenarios (`docs/verification.md`).
- Added a dependency-free native session-mode extension: `--no-session` uses per-turn meaningful learner-record updates only with explicit selection and ongoing saving consent; normal sessions batch updates at checkpoints. Teaching reuses loaded instructions and records, avoids rewriting unchanged profiles, and keeps pending evidence in a compact conversation ledger. Explicit cadence preferences and immediate save/correction requests remain available; unknown mode falls back to checkpoints. Abrupt exits can lose unflushed changes. Reasoning, evidence checks, and privacy requirements are unchanged. See `docs/verification.md` for exercised CLI scenarios and limits.

## 2026-10-27

- Added a native, dependency-free `.omp/extensions/study-clock.js` and the `/teach-schedule` command / **Schedule** section of `adaptive-tutor`. Each turn receives the host's local time and any active block, and the `study_clock` tool plans, begins, extends, ends, and lists timed study blocks.
- Before each task, `task_start` applies a calibration factor learned from that learner's past actual/estimate ratios and reports whether the task fits. A task that does not fit (or leaves under 10 minutes) is the last task; `task_rescope` records the agreed smaller scope. A per-minute in-session check notifies at 15/5 minutes left, on task overrun or spill, at block end, and when a planned block is due.
- Schedules are session-only by default. With an explicit alias, an existing profile, and consent covering the file, they persist in `.teacher/learners/<alias>/schedule.json`. Schedule data is planning data, not learning evidence, and nothing runs while OMP is closed. Verified with a stub harness and real CLI scenarios using a fictional alias (`docs/verification.md`).
- Fixed a cost regression found by the new `evals/study-clock/` overhead eval: the first clock put the changing time in the system prompt, which rewrote the prompt cache every turn (cache hit on later turns 0.21 vs 0.96; about 3.7× uncached input tokens). The clock reading now travels as a hidden per-turn message, with a constant system note. Measured result: same cache hit, tokens, and requests as no clock for ordinary lessons; wall time within noise; about 0.5 ms local CPU per tick or turn. Results and limits are in `docs/verification.md`.
- The overhead eval now measures teaching-turn cost: seconds to first visible text, approximate hidden reasoning tokens, teaching characters, and the share of time/schedule talk. It adds a `block-lesson` scenario with the same learner turns inside an active block and compares full `.omp/` snapshots. Its first run found the model doing clock bookkeeping between exercises (2 of 9 teaching turns had clock tool calls). Fixes: `plan` can start the first task, `task_next` combines end and start, and the Schedule skill treats tasks as 15+ minute segments, takes time from the injected message, and mentions time only when it changes what the learner should do. After the change: 0/9 teaching turns with clock calls, no time talk, turn times within noise of no clock, one clock call to open a block (was 2). See `docs/verification.md`.
- Added weekly study plans to the study clock (`plan_week`, `plan_view`, `plan_item`, `plan_status`, `begin` with `planId`). Plans have recurring Monday–Sunday slots and run until a date or until every topic is done. Topics are queued in study order with hour budgets. A slot's subject is a preference (or exclusive with `strict`), topics can be limited to certain days, and `after` puts prerequisites first. Several topics share a slot. The calendar and per-course finish dates are projected from now and from minutes actually studied, so missed or extra sessions move the dates, and `until` reports any deficit with the extra minutes per week needed. Today's session appears in the per-turn clock message, a notice fires when it is due, and ending the block credits the studied topics. Added `evals/study-clock/plan-check.mjs` (deterministic checks). The overhead eval shows no regression in teaching turns.

## 2026-10-28

- Added native `pause` and `resume` actions to the shared study-clock extension. Breaks preserve the block/task and remaining study time, shift deadlines on resume, suppress paused timer warnings, and are excluded from task calibration and weekly study credit. Paused schedules persist under the existing consent rules; weekly slots are not automatically rescheduled.
- Updated the scheduling workflow and command to use real pauses instead of closing and recreating blocks.
- Clarified that stopped lessons continue from their unfinished learning checkpoint in the next scheduled block of the same subject, without promising a return date or leaving a timer paused.

## 2026-10-30

- Feedback is candid and leaves the repair to the learner. After an attempt the tutor gives the verdict first, then locates and classifies each consequential error (location with a short quote, error type, consequence), without softening, consolation, or praise the evidence does not support. While a revision remains it does not write corrected values, steps, statements, terms, checks, or answers; small precision fixes are flagged, not supplied. Solutions come on explicit request or when no revision remains, and later success on that item is recorded as supported. The rule is in `.omp/RULES.md`, `.omp/SYSTEM.md`, `adaptive-tutor` (Lesson, Practice/homework, Feedback), `/teach-feedback`, the programming subject profile, `language-coach`, and `docs/teaching.md`.
- Added an optional, learner-defined **Grading** section to `adaptive-tutor`: when the learner's plan records a scheme (scale and percentage table with its source, resubmission rule, single-attempt exams, course weighting, verdict format), assignments announce points per criterion, each submission is scored per criterion with checked arithmetic, and items whose fix was requested or revealed are frozen. Without a scheme, no grades. Templates gained matching optional fields.
- Added `evals/feedback-candor/`: real-CLI scenarios that check for leaked fixes and cushioning phrases, with a control that an explicit solution request is still answered. Against the previous configuration, on the reported trigger ("can you review my homework?" on a multi-part assignment), substantive fixes went from 3/3 runs to 0/3; cushioning phrases from 4/8 to 0/15 turns. Results and limits are in `docs/verification.md`.
- `.omp/config.yml` allows `shasum <file>` (any options) through `bash` without an approval prompt, for checking whether a submission changed since it was graded. Chained or piped commands still prompt. Checks in `docs/verification.md`.
- Removed mandatory learner self-assessment and formal homework closure. The tutor now owns evaluation structure, grading, and its justification; F is tutor feedback, not another learner deliverable. Help disclosure is conversational, administrative closure earns/loses no points, and missing closure cannot block advancement. Updated the homework/assignment templates, practice and Typst commands, writing guidance, teaching/evidence documentation, and current-policy feedback fixture. Subject reasoning, checks, prerequisite evidence, and answer-request safeguards remain. Native CLI checks and limits are in `docs/verification.md`; no learner data is included in the tests or this change log.
- Added a tutor-written **"before you submit"** check to homework: two fixed questions (every part answered? every requested check done?) plus one task-specific question from the rubric and earlier omissions. It is a one-minute mental pass: nothing is written, submitted, or graded. Grading now records points lost to omissions separately from conceptual, execution, and notation errors, so each learner's own data shows whether the check helps; it is revised or dropped if omissions do not fall. Updated the sticky rule, `adaptive-tutor`, `/teach-practice`, `/teach-typst`, the Typst/assignment/progress templates, README, `docs/teaching.md`, and `docs/homework-evidence.md` (new sources S15–S16, claim C12, labeled as a design inference). `evals/feedback-candor/` gained `presubmit-homework`, `closure-progress`, and `missing-reasoning`, and its solution-control pattern now accepts compact TeX fractions. Results in `docs/verification.md`.
